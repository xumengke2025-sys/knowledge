## solid-state-battery-Q1

## solid-state-battery-Q2

分类起点：H01M4

概念式：`(lithium metal OR cathode coating OR 界面包覆) AND (对应IPC/CPC分类)`

筛选：必须关联固态电解质或固态电芯
